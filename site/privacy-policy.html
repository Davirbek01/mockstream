Mock Stream — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Mock Stream ("we", "us") provides English exam practice (CEFR Multilevel and IELTS) on the web and in our mobile app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It applies to the Mock Stream mobile app and website.

Information we collect

  • Account & identity — when you sign in with Apple, Google, or Telegram we receive your name, email address, and/or Telegram username and ID. If you continue as a guest, we store only the display name you enter, on your device.
  • Device identifier — a device ID is used to enforce free-trial limits and deliver push notifications. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Practice content & results — your exam answers, scores, levels/bands, and the date/time of attempts, so you can see your results and progress.
  • Audio recordings — for Speaking practice, your microphone audio is recorded on-device and uploaded solely to transcribe and grade your spoken answers. Microphone access is requested only when you start a Speaking task.
  • Weekly League entry — if you take a mock or practice test while signed in, an entry is created for the in-app Weekly League: your first name and the initial of your last name, your XP total, and the number of mocks you completed. See “Weekly League” below.
  • Activation codes — codes you enter to unlock mocks are validated against our servers.
  • Notification token — a push token (via Apple/Google push services) so we can send result-ready and new-mock notifications you opt into.

How we use it

  • Authenticate you and sync results across your devices.
  • Score your answers and generate AI feedback for Writing and Speaking.
  • Enforce free-trial and premium/code access.
  • Send notifications and optional daily practice reminders (you can turn these off in Settings or your OS).

Service providers we share data with

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage (results, reports, audio).
  • Apple, Google & Telegram — sign-in providers (only when you choose them).
  • AI grading providers (e.g. DeepSeek, OpenAI, and similar), accessed through our secure proxy — your answer text/transcript is sent for scoring and feedback. We do not sell your data.
  • Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver notifications.

Weekly League (leaderboard)

The app includes a Weekly League — a rolling 7-day ranking that encourages regular practice. When you complete a mock or a practice test while signed in, that activity is uploaded to our server and used to build the ranking.

  • What other candidates see: your first name and the initial of your last name (for example “Aziza K.”), your XP total, and how many mocks you finished. Nothing else.
  • What is never shown: your email address, your Telegram username or ID, your device identifier, your answers, your audio recordings, your band/level scores, and your individual reports. These stay private to your account.
  • Who can see it: the board is limited to candidates of your own learning centre, not the whole public internet, and it is visible only inside the app.
  • Opting out: the League is part of the practice experience, but you do not have to appear on it. Email us at the address below and we will exclude your account from the ranking; your results and reports remain available to you as normal. Practising while signed out (guest mode) also keeps you off the board.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and results until you delete them. You can delete your account and associated data from within the app (Settings → Delete account), or by emailing us at the address below. Deleting your account removes your authentication record and your stored results; backups are purged on our normal cycle.

Children

Mock Stream is intended for exam candidates (generally teenagers and adults preparing for English exams). It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Use guest mode to avoid creating an account.
  • Turn off notifications and reminders in Settings or your device settings.
  • Decline microphone access (Speaking practice won't be available without it).
  • Ask us to remove you from the Weekly League ranking (see above).
  • Delete your account at any time.
