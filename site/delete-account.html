Delete your Mock Stream account & data

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Mock Stream account (the English exam-practice app for CEFR Multilevel and IELTS) and the personal data associated with it.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Mock Stream app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email davirbekkhasanov02@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with (or include your Telegram username), with the subject "Delete my Mock Stream account". We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your authentication record (name, email and/or Telegram username and ID).
  • Your exam answers, scores, levels/bands, and attempt history.
  • Your Speaking audio recordings and generated reports.
  • Your push-notification token and reminder settings.

What may be kept

We may retain limited records required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, abuse logs), and anonymised, non-identifying usage statistics that are not linked to you. Routine encrypted backups are purged on our normal cycle (within 30 days).

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: davirbekkhasanov02@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
