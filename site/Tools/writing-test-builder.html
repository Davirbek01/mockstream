Dear Resident, Our Neighborhood Office has launched a new safety programme including CCTV cameras, evening workshops, and lighting improvements. We want your feedback! What do you think? Do you have any concerns or suggestions? Please write to us. Neighborhood Manager

Task 1.1 50–70 words

Write a note to your friend. Tell your friend about the safety programme and give your opinion. Write about 50 words.

Task 1.2 120–150 words

Write a letter to the Neighborhood Office manager. Explain your opinion on the programme and suggest one or two improvements. Write 120–150 words.
