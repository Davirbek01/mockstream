Help Center

We're a small team and we read every message. Reach out via Telegram and we'll get back to you — usually within a few hours.

@mrkhasanoff3 Open in Telegram →

Common questions

  • VIP / Premium code — Enter it in the VIP Activation box at the top of this menu. Premium unlocks AI feedback on every skill; Regular gives session access.
  • Lost results — Sign in with Google (or Telegram, where available) so your attempts sync across devices. Guest results stay only on the current device.
  • Mic / audio issues — Allow microphone permission when the browser prompts; if you previously denied it, open site settings and reset.
  • Mock not loading — Try a hard refresh, then sign out and back in. If it still fails, message us with the mock number.

Other ways to reach us

Telegram is the fastest channel. For urgent issues during an exam, send a screenshot of the page along with your message — it cuts the back-and-forth in half.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 23 May 2026

By using Mock Stream you agree to the terms below. They're written in plain language; if anything is unclear, message us on Telegram (@mrkhasanoff3).

1. Who we are

Mock Stream is a practice platform for CEFR, IELTS and Cambridge English exams. We provide mock tests, AI feedback, and progress tracking. We're independent of Cambridge Assessment English, IELTS, and any official examining body.

2. Accounts

You can use Mock Stream as a Guest, or sign in with Google / Telegram. A signed-in account syncs your results across devices. You're responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials secure.

3. VIP and premium access

Premium and Regular VIP codes are issued individually and may be revoked if shared, resold, or used in bad faith. Your premium status applies to the account it was activated on; refunds are handled case-by-case via Telegram.

Subscription period. Each premium/regular access is granted for a fixed period (90 days by default; the actual length is set by your administrator at the time of purchase). When the period ends, premium features are automatically disabled. You can request a renewal from your administrator.

Device limit. A single account can be used on up to 5 different devices. To enforce this and prevent account sharing, we record a device identifier the first time you sign in on each browser/device. We do not share this data with third parties, do not use it for advertising, and use it only to protect the subscription you paid for. If you genuinely need to use more than 5 devices, contact your administrator.

4. Content and fair use

Mock content, audio, AI explanations and example answers are for personal study only. Don't copy or redistribute them. Recordings you make during a mock are stored to score your attempt and may be reviewed by our team for quality checks; we don't sell them to third parties.

5. Acceptable behaviour

Don't attempt to break the platform, scrape mocks at scale, or harass other students or staff. Accounts that do so will be locked.

6. AI feedback

AI scores and feedback are estimates intended to help you improve — they aren't official exam results. We do our best to keep them accurate but they should not be used as proof of proficiency to any third party.

7. Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run the service: your name, your sign-in email if you choose one, attempts and scores. We don't sell personal data. Contact us via Telegram to request a copy of your data or to delete your account.

8. Service availability

The service is provided "as is". We aim for high uptime but can't guarantee uninterrupted access. We may update features, mocks, and pricing without prior notice.

9. Changes to these terms

If we make meaningful changes we'll update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, post a notice on the landing page.

10. Contact

Questions, complaints, or feedback: @mrkhasanoff3 on Telegram.
