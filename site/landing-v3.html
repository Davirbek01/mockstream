Help Center

We're a small team and we read every message. Reach out via Telegram and we'll get back to you — usually within a few hours.

@mrkhasanoff3 Open in Telegram →

Common questions

  • VIP / Premium code — Enter it in the VIP Activation box at the top of this menu. Premium unlocks AI feedback on every skill; Regular gives session access.
  • Lost results — Sign in with Google (or Telegram, where available) so your attempts sync across devices. Guest results stay only on the current device.
  • Mic / audio issues — Allow microphone permission when the browser prompts; if you previously denied it, open site settings and reset.
  • Mock not loading — Try a hard refresh, then sign out and back in. If it still fails, message us with the mock number.

Other ways to reach us

Telegram is the fastest channel. For urgent issues during an exam, send a screenshot of the page along with your message — it cuts the back-and-forth in half.
